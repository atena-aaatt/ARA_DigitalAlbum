ARA GALLERY — DIGITAL ALBUM

This folder contains a static HTML album made from the supplied PDF.

To publish for free with Netlify:
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Sign in / create a free Netlify account.
3. Drag the ENTIRE "ara_gallery_album" folder (or upload the ZIP) to the page.
4. Netlify will give you a free *.netlify.app address.
5. Use that address to make your QR code.

Do not rename or remove the "pages" folder or index.html.
